Static evidence (RhakMu virtual addresses)
  4D9826/4D982F initialize classSCREEN width/height at 0106A5E0/0106A5E2
  to 1024/768. 4D98D9 invokes iCARUS_Init@32 with 16-bit color and these
  dimensions for both physical and virtual buffers.
  4DC480 classSCREEN::ResolutionChange reinitializes iCARUS and calls
  CPannelMgr::ChangeResAction (465830). Scene paths request BOTH 800x600 and
  1024x768; a single initial-global edit is insufficient.
  4DC1EF..4DC244 choose RGB565/RGB555/reversed masks from queried pixel format.
  iCARUS.dll imports DirectDrawCreateEx. Its iCARUS_Init is 10017ED0;
  10017FC3..10017FCE divides byte pitch by bytes/pixel. RhakMu Draw_Tile uses
  engine pixels at tagICARUS+70 and row-offset table +74 (4CA766..4CA784).
  Runtime baseline observed at 800x600: RGB565, 16 bits/pixel, byte pitch 1600,
  pixel pitch 800; physical/virtual dimensions both 800x600.
  4CA690 Draw_Tile derives columns = width/32, rows = (height-152)/32.
  This is integer division, so 720 introduces a partial bottom strip that
  cannot be solved by merely increasing the surface dimensions.
  World tile stride is 0x208, independent of screen width; do not replace it.
  46732D..4673A8 minimap camera extents derive from the above tile dimensions
  and map dimensions. No simple 16:9 scale factor should replace projection.
  4D7F20 camera recenter/boundary similarly uses width and height-152.
  4DC3FA..4DC469 large-mode HUD has fixed left/right margins 112/113 and
  clipping constants 1024/768. Child controls have individual ChangeRes methods.
  435550 MakeSkyDrawList, 43E520 Object_MakeDrawList, 4CC520 Draw_SKYCharacter,
  4CE470 Draw_GNDCharacter, 4CFEE0 Draw_GNDConstruct,
  4D23F0/4D28F0/4D2960 magic/bullets, 4CAF40 Draw_WarFog, 4CA960 Draw_Rain,
  4D3B50 construction preview, 4D41B0 drag box, 4D4440 cursor:
  identified candidates, NOT fully verified/fixed.
